# Design review: edge instruments (DESIGN, HIPPO, PARK, 02, 04, 05, 02+04)

The findings fall into three sections, in order of severity:

- **Part A** covers genuine contradictions, where two passages give different verdicts for the same scenario.
- **Part B** covers doctrine rules that nothing underneath enforces. Per your brief, these carry the same severity as Part A.
- **Part C** covers unresolved choices and ambiguities. They are not contradictions yet. They are where contradictions will appear once a value is picked.

---

## PART A — Contradictions

### A1. 02 gives two different halt rules against the learned conv

- **02 / Meter:** "Halt if the free conv beats the frozen bank by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\). Ties are Halt."
- **02 / Halt:** "Halt: the frozen bank does not beat the learned first conv / patch+layer-1 by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\)"

**Trigger.** The bank beats the conv by less than \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\), or the conv beats the bank by less than \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\).

- The Meter rule does not fire, because the conv did not beat the bank by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\).
- The Halt rule does fire.

The Meter sentence puts the burden on the competitor. The Halt sentence and DESIGN put it on the instrument.

**Fix.** In 02 / Meter, replace the sentence with: "Halt if the frozen bank does not beat the free conv by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\)."

### A2. 02's doctrine-5 control only needs \(M_{\mathrm{match}}\) to pass, but DESIGN requires \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\) against every comparator

- **DESIGN / Margins:** "Ties are Halt, not Stop — Stop requires beating **every named comparator** by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\)."
- **02 / Halt (doctrine 5):** "a linear map … on raw patches matches or beats the bank within \(M_{\mathrm{match}}\)."
- **02 / Stop:** "including the bank beating the learned conv by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\) and \(\varepsilon\ldots\) holding". The raw-patch linear map is not listed.

**Trigger.** \(M_{\mathrm{beat}} > M_{\mathrm{match}}\), and the bank beats the raw-patch linear map by an amount between the two margins.

- Under 02, no halt fires, so 02 reaches Stop.
- Under DESIGN, this outcome is Halt.

**Fix.** Add "and the raw-patch linear map" to the list of things 02 / Stop requires the bank to beat by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\). Also add a matching halt line.

### A3. Doctrine 5 defines "matches or beats" with \(M_{\mathrm{match}}\), but 04 and 05 attach that label to an \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\) halt

- **DESIGN / Doctrine 5:** "the control matches or beats the instrument (the instrument fails to beat the control by more than \(M_{\mathrm{match}}\))."
- **05 / Halt:** "does not beat the \(N\)-tap FIR by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\) … (Doctrine 5: FIR matches or beats the stem.)"
- **04 / Halt:** "does not beat the control by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\) on ARI. (… Doctrine 5: control matches or beats the instrument.)"

**Trigger.** \(M_{\mathrm{beat}} > M_{\mathrm{match}}\), and the instrument beats the control by an amount between the two margins.

- The halt fires correctly.
- However, its recorded reason (`labkit why`) says "readout does the job".
- Under the doctrine's own definition, the readout did not do the job. This is the wrong diagnosis, and it will steer the decision about whether a warehouse variant is warranted.

**Fix.** Split each of these into two lines:

1. A doctrine-5 halt at \(M_{\mathrm{match}}\).
2. A "did not clear the superiority margin" halt at \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\).

Alternatively, change both parentheticals to "(Ties are Halt; superiority margin not met)".

### A4. Classical-bank parameters: whoever mints them first is blocked by the other document

- **04 / Spec:** "named frozen substitute (Gabor energy; classical-bank params are minted by **02+04 / this lock**, see LOCKS.md)"
- **02+04 / Spec:** "Classical-bank params for v0 …: **minted in this lock**"
- **02+04 / Status and DESIGN:** "If 04 is open, `v0` waits for 04's lock."
- **DESIGN / table:** "02+04 … **kick off first**"

**Trigger.** 04 is open, which is the default.

- If 02+04 mints the parameters, 04 cannot lock until 02+04 locks. But 02+04 cannot lock until 04 locks. That is a deadlock.
- If 04 mints its own parameters, 02+04 mints a second set. 02+04 then copies \(T\) and the margins from a 04 lock that used a different bank.
- In either case, "kick off first" is false whenever 04 is open.

**Fix.** Have exactly one owner.

- Recommended: 04's lock mints the classical-bank parameters. 02+04 v0 copies them verbatim along with \(T\) and the margins, and records 04's hash.
- Change DESIGN's "first" to "first composed instrument; after 04's lock if 04 is open".

### A5. DESIGN and 02+04 give different conditions for when 02+04 mints its own \(T\) and margins

- **DESIGN / Kick off:** "If the **one-module decision is on**, 02+04 states those numbers itself." The decision is on only when both GATE_35 and GATE_21 are closed.
- **02+04 / Status:** "If **04 is closed**, this lock states \(T\) and the margins itself."
- **02+04 / Spec:** "If 04 is labkit-`closed` at this lock time, mint them here."

**Trigger.** GATE_21 (04) is closed and GATE_35 (02) is not.

- Under 02+04, it mints the numbers.
- Under DESIGN, 04 is not open, so there is no wait. The decision is off, so there is no self-statement either. The case is undefined.

**Fix.** In DESIGN, change "If the one-module decision is on" to "If 04 is closed".

### A6. 02+04 v1 is permitted after v0 halts, but doctrine says a halt abandons the instrument

- **DESIGN / Doctrine 3:** "A halt **abandons the instrument**."
- **DESIGN / Doctrine 5:** "Halt means abandon."
- **02+04 / Build order:** "Second lock (`bank_wave_v1`), **only if 02 has frozen**". There is no condition on how v0 turned out.

**Trigger.** v0 fires (for example, row 4 matches row 3, so the wave is decoration). Later, 02 reaches Stop.

- Under 02+04, the build order permits v1.
- Under doctrine, `<id>` 02plus04 is abandoned.

**Fix.** Add "and v0 is not-fired" to v1's precondition. Alternatively, state in DESIGN that a halt abandons the *version*, and that a new version of the same `<id>` is allowed only for a changed stem.

### A7. "Keep or tweak" conflicts with the ban on tuning and with abandon-on-halt

- **DESIGN / Doctrine 2:** "shoot the candidate through its own bench, **keep or tweak**."
- **02, 04, 05, and 02+04 / Out of scope:** "**Tuning any knob on any data.**"
- **Doctrines 3 and 5:** a halt abandons the instrument.

**Trigger.** A run misses a margin, and someone adjusts coupling, bandwidth, or \(N\), then reruns. Doctrine 2 authorizes this. Every document underneath forbids it.

**Fix.** In doctrine 2, change "keep or tweak" to "keep or abandon (a changed knob is a new `<id>` with its own lock)".

### A8. The peek halt fires on 02+04's lock-time margin copy, and on v1's new cap

- **Peek halt (all documents):** "any meter, threshold, or margin written or changed after any bench data **for that bench, from any run**, has been seen."
- **02+04 / Spec:** "\(T\), \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\), \(M_{\mathrm{match}}\): copy 04's numbers verbatim **at this lock time**". The bench is 04's ARI bind bench.
- **02+04 / Build order:** "v0's data is visible; v1 must not change v0's margins. **It may state its own \(P_{\mathrm{frozen\_inherit}}\).**"

**Trigger 1.** 04 locks and runs. Then 02+04 v0 locks, which is the ordering DESIGN prescribes. The margins are written into 02+04's lock after ARI-bench data has been seen, so peek fires.

**Trigger 2.** v1 writes a new \(P_{\mathrm{frozen\_inherit}}\) cap, which is a threshold, after v0's data on the same bench has been seen. Peek fires.

**Fix.** Add to the peek definition: "Copying a value verbatim from an earlier lock, with its hash recorded, is not writing." Also either declare that \(P_{\mathrm{frozen\_inherit}}\) is a structural count and not a threshold, or require v1's count to be fixed at v0's lock time.

### A9. Frozen inherited weights received gradient in an earlier bench, so doctrines 2 and 3 fire on every composition

- **Doctrine 2 halt:** "any gradient reaching this block from a loss computed outside its own bench, including through … a frozen tower."
- **Doctrine 3 halt:** "any blueprint parameter receiving gradient".
- **02 / Scar:** "In any composed module it [the mix] is a **frozen blueprint layer**". It was trained on 02's invariance bench.

**Trigger.** 04 with a frozen-02 input, or 02+04 v1. The mix is a blueprint parameter of the composed block. It did receive gradient, from a loss outside the composed block's bench. Read literally, both halts fire before any run.

**Fix.** Add "during any run under this lock" to both doctrine halts.

### A10. Trajectory storage is licensed, but the delay halt treats it as buffered state

- **DESIGN / Caps:** "Trajectory storage is \(T_{\mathrm{store}}\); the lock writes a finite integer or `inf`."
- **02+04 / Spec:** \(T_{\mathrm{store}}\) is allowed.
- **DESIGN / Delay** and the halts in 04 and 02+04: "an explicit delay element or **buffered state not arising from nearest-neighbour coupling**" is a halt.

**Trigger.** \(T_{\mathrm{store}} > 0\), and the readout consumes stored past frames. That storage is buffered state that does not arise from coupling.

- Under the caps rule, it is legal within budget.
- Under the delay rule, it halts.

**Fix.** Choose one:

- In the Delay definition, add "Trajectory storage within \(T_{\mathrm{store}}\), read only for logging and never by the readout, is not a delay."
- Or force \(T_{\mathrm{store}} = 0\) for 04 and 02+04.

### A11. 02+04 Stop says "under \(P_{\max}\)", but the halt fires only above \(P_{\max}\)

- **02+04 / Halt:** "trained scar **exceeds** \(P_{\max}\)"
- **02+04 / Stop:** "with the scar **under** \(P_{\max}\)"

**Trigger.** The scar has exactly \(P_{\max}\) parameters. No halt fires, but Stop is not met. Under "any outcome that does not meet Stop is a halt", the instrument halts with no named reason.

**Fix.** In 02+04 / Stop, change "under" to "not exceeding".

### A12. DESIGN names two different authoritative texts: the LOCK.md content and the draft at the recorded hash

- **DESIGN / Authority:** "the draft **at the recorded git hash** is authoritative … **one hash**."
- **DESIGN / template:** "A `…/LOCK.md` must name, in writing, before any run: Spec, Knobs, Meter, Halt, Scar, Out of scope".
- **Doctrine 1:** the wrapper checks headings in `INSTRUMENT_DOC` (the draft), not in LOCK.md.

**Trigger.** LOCK.md restates a margin, and that value differs from the draft at the recorded hash. It is unclear which text wins, and which file the wrapper validated.

**Fix.** Choose one:

- In the template, change "must name, in writing" to "must record the draft hash; the draft at that hash must contain". LOCK.md then carries no normative text.
- Or make LOCK.md the only authoritative document and point the wrapper at it.

## PART B — Doctrine rules nothing enforces

### B1. Doctrine 4's import test cannot be evaluated, and its revocation act is never named

- **Doctrine 4:** "valid only into a successor whose **time step or pixel pitch equals the lock's**", and "a **named** labkit act fires if a successor import changes knobs or scar."

**Gap.**

- No instrument's Spec or Knobs records a pixel pitch or a physical time step. 05's \(\theta\) is "in steps". 04's lattice is a grid. 02 has "aperture / patch size".
- The revocation act is never given a name.
- Only 05 mentions the import test. 02, 04, and 02+04 are silent, even though 02+04 is explicitly built to be imported ("a frozen stem").

**Trigger.** Someone imports `bank_wave_v0` at a different resolution. Nothing can determine whether the import is valid or whether revocation should fire.

**Fix.**

- Add "pixel pitch" (to 02, 04, and 02+04) or "time step" (to 05) to the required contents of Spec in the DESIGN template.
- Name the revocation act, for example `revoke-import`, in DESIGN.
- Add a one-line import clause to each instrument, as 05 has.

### B2. Every lock must write \(T_{\mathrm{store}}\), but only 02+04 does

- **DESIGN / Caps:** "the lock writes a finite integer or `inf`."

**Gap.** 04 and 05 are stateful and never mention \(T_{\mathrm{store}}\). 04's knobs mention "state size" but not trajectory storage. The wrapper checks only that headings are non-empty.

**Trigger.** A 04 run stores the full trajectory. The DESIGN rule is violated, and no halt or wrapper check notices.

**Fix.** Add a \(T_{\mathrm{store}}\) line to the Scar sections of 04 and 05, plus the conditional halt. Better still, add \(T_{\mathrm{store}}\) to the template's Scar bullet.

### B3. 04's doctrine-3 halt lists only (\(W\), coupling) and omits the upstream bank and mix

- **Doctrine 3:** "Halt (every instrument): **any** blueprint parameter receiving gradient".
- **04 / Halt:** "any blueprint parameter **(\(W\), coupling)** receiving gradient".
- By contrast, 02+04 lists "bank finetuned; mix retrained on the bind bench".

**Trigger.** In 04 with a frozen-02 input, someone retrains the frozen 1×1 mix on the bind bench.

- The loss is inside the bench, so doctrine 2 does not fire.
- \(P_{\mathrm{frozen\_inherit}}\) checks only the count.
- The parenthetical reads as an exhaustive list, so this case escapes.

**Fix.** In 04's doctrine-3 halt, change "(\(W\), coupling)" to "(\(W\), coupling, the input bank or substitute, and any inherited mix)".

### B4. "Knobs are fixed" and "no tuning" have no halt

- **Template:** Knobs are "Fixed scalar values".
- **All documents / Out of scope:** "Tuning any knob on any data."
- **Peek halt:** covers only "meter, threshold, or margin".

**Gap.** Only 05's \(\Delta\) has a halt of the form "implemented value ≠ locked value". The coupling scale, bandwidth, lattice, \(N\), and \(T\) can drift or be retuned after data is seen, and no halt fires.

**Fix.** Add to each instrument's halts: "Halt: any implemented knob differs from its lock value, or any knob written or changed after bench data has been seen." Equivalently, extend the peek halt to cover "knob".

### B5. \(M_{\mathrm{beat}} \ge M_{\mathrm{match}}\) and the other template requirements are checked by nothing

- **DESIGN:** "Every lock must satisfy \(M_{\mathrm{beat}} \ge M_{\mathrm{match}}\)". The template also requires \(P_{\max}\) and the state budget as integers, and requires Stop to be named.
- **Doctrine 1:** the wrapper refuses only missing or empty headings.

**Trigger.** A lock writes \(M_{\mathrm{beat}} < M_{\mathrm{match}}\). An outcome can then satisfy "matches within \(M_{\mathrm{match}}\)" (halt) and "beats by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\)" at the same time. The wrapper accepts the lock.

**Fix.** Extend the wrapper to check:

- \(M_{\mathrm{beat}} \ge M_{\mathrm{match}}\)
- \(P_{\max}\) and the state budget parse as integers
- the Halt section contains a Stop paragraph

Alternatively, add a universal halt: "lock violates \(M_{\mathrm{beat}} \ge M_{\mathrm{match}}\)".

### B6. Sequencing rules have no halt

- **DESIGN, 05, and PARK:** 05 kicks off only after `02plus04/v0/LOCK.md` exists and GATE_34 is decided.
- **02+04:** v0 waits for 04's lock.

**Gap.** The provenance halt checks only that *some* LOCK.md with a hash exists. A 05 lock that runs before GATE_34 is decided fires nothing.

**Fix.** In 05, add: "Halt: bench run before `instruments/02plus04/v0/LOCK.md` exists and GATE_34 is decided." In 02+04, add the equivalent halt for 04's lock.

## PART C — Unresolved choices and ambiguities

### C-i. Explicit either/or choices and ranges

These are legal as written. The risk is the conflict each one creates once resolved.

| # | Passage (source) | What breaks once picked | Smallest resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | "output channel count … measured **before or after** a mix" (02 / Spec) | If "after", the mixed channels no longer correspond to the pre-assigned (orientation, scale) targets. "Energy-in-correct-band" loses its definition. The competitor's "same number of output channels" also changes. | Pick "before the mix", or require that the assignment be restated on the mixed channels. |
| U2 | "\(P_{\max}\) of the mix (if a mix exists) or \(P_{\max}^{\mathrm{ctrl}}\) (if no scar)" (02 / Halt); "Scar: None required" (02) | \(P_{\max}^{\mathrm{ctrl}}\) is defined nowhere, and the template requires \(P_{\max}\) as an integer. With no scar, the control's size is unbounded. | Define \(P_{\max}^{\mathrm{ctrl}}\) in DESIGN / Caps, or require \(P_{\max}\) even when it is 0 and use it for the control. |
| U3 | "learned first conv **/** ViT patch+layer-1" with "the same kernel spatial support" (02) | The ViT branch is already contradictory. Layer-1 attention is global, so it cannot share the bank's kernel support. | Drop "ViT patch+layer-1", or define its support as the patch only, with attention excluded. |
| U4 | "8 orientations, 4 scales" as a "draft default **if someone insists**"; "Aperture **/** patch size" (02) | A number with no lock status invites someone to run 02 alone before the lock. It is also unclear whether "aperture" and "patch size" are one knob or two. | Delete the defaults, or mark them "non-normative until 02 v0 lock". Name a single knob. |
| U5 | "grid size, **4- or 8-neighbour**, boundary" (04) | The boundary rule (periodic or clamped) changes wave behaviour at the edges. 02+04 copies these values, so any drift propagates. | 04's lock names all three. 02+04 copies them with 04's hash. |

Otherwise it mints them. |

| U9 | "\(T_{\mathrm{store}}\): … a finite integer **or** `inf`" (DESIGN, 02+04) | Choosing `inf` collides with the delay halt (A10). | Resolve A10 first, then pick. |
| U10 | \(T\) and margins "copied **or** minted"; lattice "04's named constants, **or** stated here" (02+04) | This depends on A5's case split. | Resolve A5. |
| U11 | "classical-bank params are minted by **02+04 / this lock**" (04) | This is the deadlock in A4. | Resolve A4. |

### C-ii. Ambiguities

**A-1. Margins are scalars, but the meters are curves or distributions.**

- 05's meter is "detection-error vs lag", which is a curve.
- 02 has three perturbation types.
- 04's ARI is measured over many images.

"Beat by \(M_{\mathrm{beat}}\)" is undefined for these. It could mean at every lag, the mean, the worst case, or with a confidence interval. Once data exists, this choice becomes a peek risk.

*Fix:* each lock states the aggregation and its units (absolute or relative).

**A-2. ARI population.** 04 says "two blob ids, overlap excluded". It does not say whether background pixels count as a third cluster or are excluded. This changes ARI substantially.

*Fix:* state "ARI over non-overlap blob pixels only" or "three labels including background".

**A-3. Halt scope over competitors.** Some halts can be read as firing on the competitors, which must be trained:

- 05: "\(A\), \(B\), or \(\Delta\) receiving gradient from **any** loss". The learned-\(A\) competitor trains \(A\).
- 02: "the filter set receiving gradient". The free conv trains its filters.

*Fix:* change the wording to "…of the instrument under test (not its named competitors)".

**A-4. Gate granularity.**

- GATE_34 is one handle covering two locks (v0 and v1). 05's kickoff condition "GATE_34 decided" is ambiguous if v0 is not-fired and v1 is fired.
- labkit stores one state per handle, while each instrument has about eight halts.

*Fix:* use one handle per version, or state that 05 keys off v0 only.

**A-5. The one-module decision is circular.** It is "on iff GATE_35 and GATE_21 are closed". Meanwhile, 02 and 04 are "labkit-closed if one-module". Nothing says what causes the closure.

*Fix:* name the act or person that closes the reserved handles, and make the decision a consequence of that act only.

**A-6. Revocation of an upstream Stop.** If 02's Stop is later revoked (doctrine 4), 04's input clause says "The lock does not change input after lock time". This leaves 04, and 02+04 v1, built on a revoked artefact with no defined outcome.

*Fix:* state whether revocation propagates, for example by closing dependent locks, or does not propagate.

**A-7. 05 kickoff when GATE_34 is closed instead of decided.** Reserved handles "may be closed without being declared". If GATE_34 is closed, 05 can never kick off.

*Fix:* change the condition to "decided or closed", or state that this outcome is intended.

**A-8. 02+04 control rows.**

- Row 2 has no readout cap. Only rows 1 and 4 are "same architecture and size cap".
- "Same architecture and size cap" is also ill-posed across inputs of different dimension (raw energy vs bank channels): a linear readout's parameter count depends on its input dimension.
- "Raw input energy, no bank" in row 1 is undefined.

*Fix:* cap all three control readouts at \(P_{\max}\), and define row 1's input explicitly.

**A-9. "The draft's git hash" is ambiguous.** It could be a commit hash or a blob hash, and it is unclear what happens with a dirty working tree.

*Fix:* specify a commit hash, and have the wrapper refuse to run with uncommitted changes to `INSTRUMENT_DOC`.

**A-10. LOCKS.md is referenced but not provided, and its authority is unclear.** It is described as holding symbol origins and "minted" classical-bank parameters. If it is a mutable top-level file, locked numbers can change without the draft hash changing.

*Fix:* state that minted values live only in the versioned LOCK.md, and that LOCKS.md is non-normative.

**A-11. Pre-lock authority covers only part of the document.** "Before a lock: the working draft is authoritative for spec / meter / halt *text*." Knobs, Scar, and Out of scope are not covered.

*Fix:* change this to "all six headings".

**A-12. 05's "length-\(L\) FIR is the ceiling".** Its role is undefined. No halt, margin, or run requirement references it.

*Fix:* either state "reported, not judged" or delete it.

**A-13. Self-referential state caps.**

- 04 lists "state size" as a knob and also halts on "state exceeds the budget".
- 05 uses \(N\) as both a knob and the cap.

Neither halt can fire unless the budget is a separate integer.

*Fix:* write the budget as a distinct integer, and require implemented state to equal the knob.

**A-14. HIPPO drift (non-normative, but misleading).**

- It lists "the timescale \(\Delta\)" among the "handful of numbers". 05 says \(\Delta\) is not a knob.
- "Input-dependent \(B,C,\Delta\) is warehouse" omits \(A\), despite the claim that "this file does not keep a second list".

*Fix:* remove \(\Delta\) from the handful of numbers, and replace the list with "see doctrine 3".

**A-15. "Label-free" vs a readout trained on blob ids (04).** "Label-free: no class id in \(W\)" permits blob-id supervision of the readout. However, "No class label in the graph" and ARI's permutation invariance leave the training target undefined.

*Fix:* the lock states the readout's loss, including how blob-id permutations are handled.
